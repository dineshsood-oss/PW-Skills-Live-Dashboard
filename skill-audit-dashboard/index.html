Control chart: daily average QA score

Centre line is the period mean; limits are ±3σ/√n for each day's audit count. Red points sit outside the limits; ringed points are part of a run of 7+ days on one side of the mean. Both signal a special cause to investigate.

Capability by category

Ppk 1.33+ is capable, 1.0–1.33 marginal, under 1.0 not capable; a negative Ppk means the average itself is under the pass mark.

How to read this lens

Every call audit is a unit; each of its 9 parameters that was scored (not NA), plus the fatal-error check, is an opportunity for a defect.

Specification

Lower spec limit (LSL) is the 70% pass mark. Scores have no upper limit, so capability is one-sided.

Ppk

(x̄ − LSL) ÷ 3σ, using the period's overall spread. Below 1.0 means a visible share of calls will keep falling under the pass mark.

Defect and DPMO

A defect is a parameter scored under 60% of its weight, or a fatal error. DPMO = defects ÷ opportunities × 1,000,000.

Sigma level

Short-term sigma from DPMO with the customary 1.5σ shift. 3σ ≈ 66,800 DPMO; 4σ ≈ 6,210; 6σ ≈ 3.4.

Rolled throughput yield

The chance a call clears every parameter first time: the product of each parameter's pass rate.

Act on it (DMAIC)

Use the Pareto to pick the vital few parameters, the control chart to spot special causes, and the QA calibration gap to keep the measurement system honest.
